In the session

Two taps
per set

Every set arrives with its weight, its reps and what you lifted last time. In RIR or RPE, with supersets, drop sets, rest-pause, and a rest timer that keeps running with the screen off.

Strength Bodyweight Timed Cardio

Periodization

The plan adapts
to what you lift

Three to five weeks with each week of intensity already worked out: accumulation, intensification and deload. The next set auto-regulates based on how you show up that day, and with Atreus Pro a coach reads your history and designs your next block.

Analytics

Numbers that tell the truth

Weekly volume per muscle group, per-exercise progression, block-by-block comparisons and a record history with the estimated 1RM behind each one. 167 exercises with technique guides, so you know what you are training and not just how much.

Privacy

Yours. Local. Portable.

Every set is written to your phone first, so the gym basement does not matter: the app works with no signal and catches up on its own once the connection is back. The most sensitive data — bodyweight, measurements, progress photos — never leaves the device. What does travel, so your history survives a change of phone, is protected with column-level permissions: only your account can read it. Details in the privacy policy.
